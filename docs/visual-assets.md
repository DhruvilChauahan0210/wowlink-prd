# WowLink cinematic visuals

Generated with the built-in image generation tool using the imagegen skill. These are original concept images, not photographs of customer events. Final assets are served locally from `public/media/`. JPEG conversion reduces delivery size only.

## Prompt set

All prompts use `photorealistic-natural`, request editorial event photography, and prohibit text, lettering, people, logos, and watermarks.

- **celebration.jpg:** A wide cinematic Indian celebration venue at blue hour, a symmetrical ivory stone arch pavilion overlooking a dark blue garden and lake, a candlelit aisle, restrained cream flowers, gauzy silk drapery framing the edges, subtle golden lights, warm champagne highlights, natural materials and dark negative space above for typography. Eye-level camera with strong depth. Landscape 16:9.
- **birthday.jpg:** A vertical 3:4 editorial photograph of a sculptural ivory cake with thin glowing candles, on a cherry-red velvet table, burgundy and blush translucent balloons, golden confetti, warm cinematic light, rich shadows and tactile event styling.
- **garba.jpg:** A vertical 3:4 editorial photograph of a Gujarati courtyard at night, a circular mandala of marigolds and pink petals, two decorated dandiya sticks in the foreground, hanging lanterns and warm golden lights, saffron and rose textiles, authentic materials and rich shadows.
- **opening.jpg:** A vertical 3:4 architectural photograph of elegant dark green boutique entrance doors, a wide champagne silk ribbon tied across the entrance, warm golden light through ribbed glass, a restrained bouquet of white flowers, refined evening lighting.
